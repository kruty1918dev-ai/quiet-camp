# Quiet Camp biome audio

Owned procedural audio generated by `tools/biome_audio.py`, deterministic seeds,
48 kHz PCM masters. No downloaded samples or third-party audio licenses.

Five seamless 12-second beds: forest, meadow, autumn, winter, shoreline water.
Three mono surface-contact variants each: snow, leaves, mud, soil, grass.

Loops stream as Vorbis; one-shots load as PCM. Preserve sample rate, disable
normalization and Doppler. Rain, tent fabric, music and fire retain their separate
existing layers. The biome beds are deliberately quiet and crossfade in over four
seconds. See `Design/Atmosphere/BIOME-SURFACES-UA.md` for routing and budgets.

`python3 tools/biome_audio.py --audit` verifies PCM levels, DC and seams. It does
not establish perceived quality or mobile performance without listening tests.
