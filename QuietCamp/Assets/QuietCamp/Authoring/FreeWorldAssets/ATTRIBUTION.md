# Free authoring sources

Kenney Nature Kit and Survival Kit: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/, CC0-1.0. Original licenses and OBJ/MTL files are retained in Editor/FreeWorldSources. Derived palette geometry uses metric sizing and muted colours.

UnityMeshSimplifier: Mattias Edlund / Whinarn, MIT; upstream revision 53fdb3122645bcd3ad2c258235200dff3dfbaa9b.

EzySlice: David Arayan, MIT; upstream revision 073efdcaf595a25f1086bdc5b7a23335a80a6755. Convex meshes only; no vertex-colour preservation in the upstream slicer.

ProBuilder 6.1.3 and Polybrush 1.2.1: Unity registry packages, subject to their included Unity package licenses.
